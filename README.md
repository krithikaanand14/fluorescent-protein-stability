# Fluorescent Protein Thermal Stability

**Research internship portfolio | Krithika Anand | Chemical Biotechnology**

A high-level overview of laboratory experience investigating fluorescent protein stability under thermal stress. This repository describes the research question, broad workflow, and technical skills without disclosing experimental datasets or internal research details.

## Research question

How can the surrounding formulation environment support the thermal stability of fluorescent proteins?

## Project overview

The internship explored the influence of additives and polymer matrices on fluorescent protein behavior under thermal stress. Work involved sample preparation, fluorescence-based measurements, and structured comparison of experimental conditions.

## My contribution

- Prepared protein samples and quantified concentrations using spectrophotometry.
- Prepared additive and polymer-matrix formulations for comparative screening.
- Carried out thermal stability measurements using qPCR-based fluorescence monitoring.
- Organized measurements and compared stability trends across conditions.
- Maintained experimental records and documented procedures and observations.

## Workflow at a glance

Sample preparation → formulation screening → thermal fluorescence measurements → data organization → comparative interpretation

This is a conceptual overview, not an experimental protocol.

## Skills demonstrated

Protein handling · Spectrophotometry · Fluorescence measurements · Experimental planning · Data interpretation · Laboratory documentation

## Scientific perspective

Protein stability is influenced by its environment. A careful assessment considers measurement quality, comparable controls, and the limits of fluorescence as a readout. No project-specific performance claims or rankings are disclosed here.
